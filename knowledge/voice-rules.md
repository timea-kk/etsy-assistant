# Voice rules: Pattern Mind Design

## Tone
- Direct, specific, honest. Dry humour is fine.
- Personal, but not overly casual.
- Confident without hype.
- Describe what the product does and what's in it. Let specifics do the selling.

## Sentence length
- Listings: short and medium sentences.
- Personal copy (About, bio): full sentences. Avoid strings of very short sentences.
- Never write three-word sentences as filler.

## Never use
- Em dashes
- "genuinely," "honestly," "straightforward," "that being said," "it's worth noting," "to be clear"
- "leverage," "game-changer," "transformative," "unlock," "arguably," "some might say"
- "beautiful," "amazing," "stunning," "gorgeous"
- Opening with "So," or "Great question"
- Closing with an offer to help further
- Hashtags on Pinterest

## Always
- Say "hand-drawn, no AI" as a plain statement, not a slogan.
- Use exact counts: "30 leaf brushes," not "lots of leaves."
- Sentence case in descriptions. Title Case in Etsy titles.

## Timea's own lines
- Don't rewrite approved lines. When she asks for additions, add before or after.
- She wants her own story, not generic copy. Ask questions when you need detail.
- Don't mention Romania, the university name, or the city she studied in.
- Mention AuDHD only when she asks for it in the text.
- Don't add personal claims she hasn't made. Example: don't say "full-time artist" unless she says it's true.
