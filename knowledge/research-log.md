# Research log

Summary of what was researched, when, and how reliable it is. Re-check items marked as secondary.

## Etsy listing rules (Nov 2026)
- Source types: Etsy Seller Handbook pages (Keywords 101, Add Attributes, Create Listings That Convert, customer service and search ranking), April 2026 title guidance (via secondary guides), and secondary SEO guides.
- Some Seller Handbook pages were marked "as of August 2025." Re-check.
- Findings: title up to 140 characters, aim for under 15 words; 13 tags of 20 characters; categories act like tags; attributes create filters; description read by search; customer service affects ranking.

## Etsy policies (Nov 2026)
- Source: Etsy Seller Policy and Advertising & Marketing Policy. Versions in the search results were effective through July 2026 and updated June 2026.
- Findings: coupons at seller discretion; Targeted Offers for favourited items, abandoned carts, and Thank-You coupons; coupons can be added to Messages; restrictions on mailing lists and buyer identity without consent.
- Conflict: one page said Etsy's policy changed on 9 July 2026. Confirm which version is live.

## Etsy API (Nov 2026)
- Official developer site could not be fetched directly. Findings come from secondary sources (connector docs, Python SDK, a third-party how-to guide).
- Confirmed by secondary sources: OAuth 2.0 with scopes; listings_w needed to create listings; POST to create draft listing; PUT to update.
- Not confirmed: digital listing type, file upload endpoints, rate limits, app approval, automation terms.

## Pinterest rules (Nov 2026)
- Source: secondary Pinterest SEO guides (2025 and 2026). Not Pinterest's own pages.
- Findings: 2:3 pins at 1000 x 1500 px; title up to 100 characters; description 100 to 500 characters (Timea's rule is 200 to 300); tagged topics up to 10 chosen in the editor; hashtags not used for discovery; text overlay in the top half.
- Re-check against Pinterest's help pages.

## Tag research (Nov 2026)
- Source: third-party tag analysis tool. Scores and KD are the tool's values.
- Competitor analysis: Pencil Twist (Slovenia) uses procreate brushes, procreate stamps, hand drawn, digital download, instant download, and product-specific tags. Her shop structure informs the anchor approach.
- Other competitor listings checked in the tool: Christmas brushes listings with snow, ice, texture, and procreate terms.
- Limitation: eRank is not used. Scores are not directly comparable to eRank values.

## Stats (Nov 2, 2026)
- Source: Etsy Stats screens (all time and last 30 days), Listing stats per listing, and traffic sources.
- Limitation: Etsy does not show which tag brought each sale. Search term data only.

## Market research: Valentine's Procreate (Nov 2026)
- Source: Etsy search results for Valentine's and Procreate terms.
- Findings: heart stamp bundles are saturated; flash tattoo, card creators, and candy hearts are crowded; some rose and flower stamps exist; few folk or botanical Valentine sets.
- Limitation: sales counts were not visible. Favourite counts ranged from low to about 70 on the listings checked. Listing dates in search results were inconsistent.

## Business and legal (Nov 2026, not yet verified)
- Impressum: required for commercial sites targeting Germany. Include name, serviceable address, and email. Registration details if applicable.
- Privacy policy (Datenschutzerklärung): required for any site or email list.
- Cancellation rules for digital products: check before writing terms.
- VAT: EU-wide €10,000 threshold for B2C sales to other EU countries. Germany's small-business (Kleinunternehmer) rule has its own thresholds. Confirm with a tax adviser.
