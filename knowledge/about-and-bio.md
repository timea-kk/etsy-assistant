## Current approved shop bio

I'm Timea, a traditionally trained artist based in Berlin, working in tech by day and drawing by hand every other hour I can find. I'm AuDHD, and it shapes how I work. I like tools that are predictable and don't pull my attention away from the drawing, so that's what I aim for in the brushes.

Pattern Mind Design is where I share the brushes and stamps I draw by hand in Procreate. Each one is made from scratch, with no AI at any stage.

I make these so you can skip the blank page and start with something already alive. Some brushes come with built-in structure, so you can build a composition faster and spend your time on the fun part. Creating should feel like play, and that's what I'm making room for.

By purchasing my brushes and tools, you're supporting my journey to becoming a full-time artist.

Happy drawing,
Timea
