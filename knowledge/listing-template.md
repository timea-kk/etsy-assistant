# Etsy listing template

Follow this order every time.

## 1. TITLE
- Up to 140 characters. Aim for fewer than 15 words.
- Lead with the main search phrase.
- Shop format: [Main phrase] | [Count and descriptor]
- Example: Cozy Christmas Procreate Brushes & Stamps | 212 Hand-Drawn Winter Elements
- No repeated phrases.
- State the character count.

## 2. DESCRIPTION
Paragraph 1: what the product is, with the count and main keywords. The first two sentences matter most. End the paragraph with "Every element is hand-drawn in Procreate. No AI involved."

Paragraph 2: where the designs come from and what makes them distinct. Concrete, no vague praise.

Paragraph 3 (optional): only if it gives the buyer information they need.

## 3. WHAT YOU GET
Emoji list with specific counts, taken from the actual contents.
Example:
🎨 50 leaf brushes
🖼️ 20 frame stamps

## 4. PERFECT FOR CREATING
Five use cases, one emoji each.

## 5. COMPATIBLE WITH
- Procreate (iPad)

## 6. TERMS OF USE
Use this wording unless Timea changes it:
✅ Use these brushes and stamps for personal and commercial projects.
✅ Create and sell finished designs and patterns using these elements.
✅ Create up to 500 end products using this brush set.
🚫 Do not resell, redistribute, or share the brush and stamp files.
🚫 Do not claim ownership of the illustrations or present them as your own.
🚫 Do not alter and sell the brushes/stamps as standalone products.

## 7. CLOSING LINE
Hand-drawn in Procreate. No AI.

## 8. SHOPPING BASKET SUMMARY
- Maximum 200 characters. State the count.
- Format: [Product name] with [key feature]. [What's included]. Instant digital download. Hand-drawn, no AI.
- Check the character count before showing the draft.

## 9. TAGS
- Exactly 13. Each 20 characters or fewer. Count each one.
- Anchor phrases: procreate brushes or procreate stamps, hand drawn, digital download, instant download. Flag digital download and instant download as weak (see tag-scores.md).
- Other tags: product-specific, multi-word, not repeating title phrases.
- Don't repeat a category or attribute as a tag.
